Derailed is a first-person co-op game for 2 to 6 players that I'm making with a few friends. You're the whole crew of TrainCorp's first train across Newlandia: punch tickets, run the stations, keep the cafe, casino, spa and boxing ring going, and deal with whatever the line throws at you. That includes a herd on the track that only a conga line will move, a Tunnel Troll who wants a hand-shadow show, a greased pig in the aisle, and track that just stops.

The line is generated as you ride it, and almost everything aboard can be picked up and thrown, including the passengers. It's built in Unity, and it isn't out yet.

## Across Newlandia

The line is generated as you ride it, from the run's seed. Every biome has its own land, trees, rocks, bridges and weather.
