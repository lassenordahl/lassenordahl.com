title: "Norwegian Cinnamon Bun Dough"
date: "2026-10-06"
---

## Ingredients

- 600 g (4 cups) white bread flour
- 110 g (½ cup) caster sugar
- 6 g (1 tsp) fine salt
- 14 g (4 tsp) instant dried yeast
- 1 egg, at room temperature
- 375 ml (1½ cups) milk, warmed to 37°C / 98°F
- 60 g (4 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened

## Steps

1. Measure the flour, sugar, salt, and yeast into the bowl of a stand mixer.
2. Lightly mix with the dough hook.
3. Add the egg.
4. Slowly pour in the warm milk and mix until it comes together into a rough dough.
5. Add the butter one tablespoon at a time, waiting until each piece is fully incorporated before adding the next.
6. Knead on medium speed for 15 to 20 minutes, until the dough is soft and smooth and no longer sticks to the side of the bowl. It will stay somewhat sticky from the butter and egg.
7. Lightly oil a large bowl, put the dough in, and cover.
8. Leave it somewhere warm for 1 to 1½ hours, until doubled.

## Notes

- Strong white bread flour works best.
- Instant yeast goes straight into the flour. Active dry yeast needs activating in warm liquid first. Fresh yeast: 40 g equals 14 g instant — crumble it into the warm milk.
- To start the night before, make the dough, cover it, and proof in the fridge for 8 to 10 hours, until at least doubled.
