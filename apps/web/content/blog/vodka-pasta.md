# Background

I use this vodka pasta recipe a lot.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/3 yellow onion, minced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/3 tsp kosher salt
- 6 oz tomato paste
- 1/3 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- 3 fl oz vodka
- 1 1/3 cups heavy cream
- 1 lb pasta (tubular preferred)
- 1/2 cup reserved pasta water
- 2 oz Parmesan-Reggiano, finely grated
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, cold
- 1 tbsp Italian parsley, minced (garnish)

## Steps

1. Warm the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium-low. Add the onion, garlic, and salt. Cook until softened.
2. Stir in the tomato paste and red pepper flakes. Cook until the paste turns a very dark red.
3. Pour in the vodka and let it sizzle off.
4. Add the cream and stir until smooth. Turn off the heat — the sauce waits here until the pasta is ready.
5. Boil the pasta in heavily salted water (as salty as the sea) to 1 minute shy of al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water, then drain.
6. Turn the sauce back to medium. Stir in the pasta water and Parmesan. Taste and salt as needed.
7. Add the pasta and cold butter. Stir vigorously until thick and glossy — this is what makes the sauce luscious, so don't rush it.
8. Top with extra Parmesan and parsley.

## Notes

- Bronze-die pasta grabs sauce better and releases more starch.
- Prep everything before you start — the sauce moves fast.
- You can skip the vodka. It helps texture but the dish still works without it.
- Cold butter is what emulsifies the sauce at the end.
